## Adversarial tests (offline, `tests/test_expect.py`, 18 tests)

The tests check that:

- A capture made after the release yields only grade-C history and never a point-in-time value.
- An old-layout page captured before the previous quarter was reported is not assigned to the next quarter.
- A split between capture and release rebases the EPS consensus.
- An expectation published one minute after the release is ignored by the link layer.
- Grade C/D rows are never usable, and the table is append-only.
- The parsers handle Meta's outlook wordings (2020, 2022–23 and implicit-year variants), Microsoft
  transcripts, and Alphabet capex statements.
- The options-implied move and reverse DCF give known answers; negative FCF returns no growth.
- The forward snapshot maps calendar months to fiscal quarters.
- The SEC contact configuration behaves correctly, and `.env` is git-ignored.
- NULL-safe deduplication works.

## Defects found and fixed during this sprint

1. **Duplicate rows.** A unique index containing a NULLable column never fires in SQLite. I found it from
   tripled rows during the audit. Fix: an expression index on `coalesce(...)`, a regression test, and the
   table rebuilt from stored evidence.
2. **Implicit-year inference.** Meta's February 2023 capex sentence has no year. The first rule took the
   nearest year in the text ("fourth quarter of 2022") and labelled 2023 capex as FY2022. Fix: inherit the
   year of the latest explicit outlook target earlier in the same release. Added an audit check that
   guidance never targets an ended period, and rebuilt the table.
3. **Alphabet "no guidance".** The scan missed capex guidance in two of three releases. Fix: a dedicated
   capex parser; absence is now recorded as "no revenue/earnings guidance; capex only".
4. **Implied-growth revision.** The change across a release mixed the new filing's cash flows with the
   price move. Fix: price-driven change (fundamentals fixed) and fundamentals effect, reported separately.
5. **Network failures.** A DNS failure aborted the archive collection. Fix: the page is skipped and logged.
6. **Yahoo archived consensus.** Inconsistent with FactSet and Zacks, and stale after the release (leakage
   trap). Rejected as a source and documented.

## What this audit does not establish

- That a dated consensus is the consensus at the exact moment of the release. Staleness is recorded:
  median capture age is small, but up to 150 days.
- That press-preview figures equal the provider's database values.
- Anything causal about the reactions.
